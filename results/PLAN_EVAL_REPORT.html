What’s At Risk

  • PRD-028 Collection Data & Persistence
    Use timestamps for sorting, sync, freshness
    The plan captures timestamps for freshness and conflict resolution, but it never specifies where those timestamps drive user-visible sorting behavior.
  • PRD-066 Ask Chat
    Answer directly with confident, spoiler-safe recommendations
    The plan defines tone, structure, and safety, but it does not encode the explicit “answer within the first 3–5 lines” acceptance bar for Ask responses.
  • PRD-078 Concepts, Explore Similar & Alchemy
    Require concept selection and guide ingredient picking
    Concept selection is required, but the plan never commits to the UX guidance that teaches users to “pick the ingredients you want more of.”
  • PRD-084 Concepts, Explore Similar & Alchemy
    Deliver surprising but defensible taste-aligned recommendations
    The plan checks reasons and rubric sampling, but it does not turn “surprising but defensible” taste alignment into a concrete prompt contract or acceptance test.
  • PRD-091 AI Voice, Persona & Quality
    Validate discovery with rubric and hard-fail integrity
    Discovery quality validation is manual and sampled, so rubric compliance and real-show integrity could regress without automatically blocking the build.

Top Gaps and Why They Matter

Risk Assessment

If the team executed this plan exactly as written, the first thing a stakeholder or QA reviewer would notice is not missing pages or broken persistence. They would notice uneven AI behavior: Ask may answer too indirectly, recommendation quality may become merely competent instead of delightfully taste-aware, and discovery regressions may slip through because the rubric is sampled rather than enforced.

Remediation Guidance

The plan mostly needs tighter specification, not more architecture. The remaining work is to convert AI quality goals and a few nuanced UX rules into explicit acceptance criteria: where timestamps drive ordering, what Ask must do in its opening lines, what guidance the concept pickers must display, and what automated or blocking checks protect discovery quality before release.
