Top Gaps And Why They Matter

PRD-033 Sync libraries/settings consistently and merge duplicates

Without explicit duplicate and settings conflict rules, users can see stale settings or collection divergence.

PRD-091 Validate discovery with rubric and hard-fail integrity

Resolvable recommendations can still regress on voice, taste, specificity, and surprise.

PRD-051 Preserve Show Detail narrative section order

The detail spec defines a reading path, not just a component list. Order affects first impression.

PRD-066 Answer directly with confident, spoiler-safe recommendations

Ask may feel personable but slow or evasive without a direct-answer bar.

PRD-076 Return bullet-only, 1-3 word, non-generic concepts

Loose formatting weakens parser reliability and the crisp concept-chip experience.
