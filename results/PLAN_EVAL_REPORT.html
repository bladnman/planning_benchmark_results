Top Gaps and Why They Matter

Critical

PRD-026 · Re-add preserves My Data and refreshes public data

The current delete flow removes the row but never explains how tags, rating, or Scoop return when the same title is saved later. That breaks collection continuity and undermines trust in the library.

Critical

PRD-072 · Exact Ask `showList` contract

The plan names a structured `{commentary, showList}` output, but it does not lock the exact `Title::externalId::mediaType;;...` parser contract. Without that, mentioned shows can fail to render or deep-link reliably.

Important

PRD-091 · Discovery rubric and hard-fail quality loop

Real-show integrity is enforced, but the broader voice, taste-alignment, and specificity rubric is not. That leaves the app open to AI drift without a clear acceptance bar.

Important

PRD-090 · Surface-specific AI context inputs

If the prompts do not receive the right library, My Data, concept, and chat context per surface, the AI will feel generic even on top of a solid backend.

Important

PRD-084 · Surprising but defensible recommendations

The plan never explains how recommendation quality will be tuned toward delightful surprise without breaking taste alignment. That is core to the product’s differentiation.

Readout for the Product Lead

Overall Posture

This is a strong implementation plan with high structural coverage. It is ready to drive build work, but it is not yet precise enough to guarantee full product-behavior parity.

Strength Clusters

Infrastructure, isolation, persistence, navigation, search, settings, and export are covered concretely. These areas are specified with tables, routes, schema decisions, and scripts.

Weakness Clusters

Gaps concentrate in AI behavior specification: exact schemas, context wiring, concept ranking, and quality validation. A smaller but meaningful gap remains in collection re-add continuity.

Most Likely Failure Mode

The app would feel solid on basic flows but generic or brittle on its AI-led discovery surfaces. QA would likely catch failures in Ask mentions, recommendation quality, and restore behavior before layout issues.
